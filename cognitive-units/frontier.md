# Cognitive Unit: Frontier

## Purpose

Explore next-generation possibilities and produce viable prototypes that could become future platform capabilities — the organization's horizon-scanning function.

---

## Outcome Focus

- **Produce 2+ viable prototypes per quarter** that could become platform capabilities (Innovation)
- **Generate 8+ documented emergence events per quarter** — unexpected insights, connections, or breakthroughs that arise from exploration (Harmonization)
- **Maintain a high ideas-generated rate** — the unit should be a consistent source of novel thinking (Innovation)
- **Feed proven patterns back to other units** — exploration only matters if it connects to production (Innovation)

---

| Metric | Category | Target | Measurement Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exploration-to-Production | Innovation | 2+ prototypes viable for production per quarter | Quarterly |

- **Beneficial functions:** [Builder](../cognitive-functions/builder.md), [Problem Framer](../cognitive-functions/problem-framer.md), [Stakeholder Harmonizer](../cognitive-functions/stakeholder-harmonizer.md)

|---|---|---|

| [Analysis Partner](../agents/analysis-partner.md) | High | Researches emerging technologies, synthesizes findings from multiple domains |

---

## Working Agreements

1. **No fixed roles for tasks** — collectively decide who (human or AI) takes each function per sprint
2. **Failure is data, not waste** — a failed prototype that teaches something is a successful exploration
3. **Thinking time is work time** — unstructured exploration and reading count as delivery
4. **AI outputs are starting points, not final answers** — AI-generated research needs human judgment on relevance
5. **Document everything, even dead ends** — future explorers will benefit from knowing what was tried
6. **Time-box exploration** — prototypes have a defined scope and deadline to prevent drift
7. **Connect to real problems** — exploration inspired by actual unit challenges is more valuable than pure speculation

## Typical Workflows

### Exploration Sprint

1. [Fresh-Eyes Observer](../cognitive-functions/fresh-eyes-observer.md) identifies an emerging technology or pattern worth exploring
2. [Analysis Partner](../agents/analysis-partner.md) researches the landscape — prior art, adjacent innovations, risks
3. [Learner](../cognitive-functions/learner.md) conducts rapid skill acquisition on the relevant technology
4. [Builder](../cognitive-functions/builder.md) + [Architecture Explorer](../agents/architecture-explorer.md) prototype a proof of concept
5. [Documentation & Knowledge](../agents/documentation-knowledge.md) captures findings and viability assessment

### Prototype-to-Production Handoff

1. [Pattern Integrator](../cognitive-functions/pattern-integrator.md) evaluates which prototype is most ready for production adoption
2. [Architecture Explorer](../agents/architecture-explorer.md) assesses integration requirements with existing platform
3. Unit presents the prototype to the receiving unit with documented constraints and open questions
4. [Documentation & Knowledge](../agents/documentation-knowledge.md) creates handoff documentation
