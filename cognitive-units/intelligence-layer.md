# Cognitive Unit: Intelligence Layer

## Purpose

Embed AI-native capabilities into the platform itself, making intelligence a built-in feature rather than a bolt-on tool — the platform's future differentiation.

---

## Outcome Focus

- **Integrate AI into 3+ platform capabilities per quarter** — AI should be woven into the platform, not sitting beside it (AI Utilization)
- **Achieve AI task sophistication Level 3+ average** — move beyond simple automation to contextual, adaptive AI behavior (AI Utilization)
- **Produce 4+ novel AI applications per quarter** — generate new ways AI can serve platform users (Innovation)
- **Increase AI usage depth across the organization** — more people using AI for more complex tasks (AI Utilization)

---

## Working Agreements

1. **No fixed roles for tasks** — collectively decide who (human or AI) takes each function per sprint
2. **Build for the platform, not for the demo** — every AI capability must work at platform scale, not just in a notebook
3. **AI outputs are starting points, not final answers** — the unit that builds AI tools must model responsible AI use
4. **Thinking time is work time** — research into new AI approaches counts as delivery
5. **Share learnings aggressively** — what this unit discovers about AI integration helps every other unit
6. **Measure sophistication, not just usage** — a widely-used but shallow AI feature is not a win
7. **Ethical considerations are first-class requirements** — bias, fairness, and transparency are not afterthoughts

## Typical Workflows

### AI Capability Integration

1. [Pattern Integrator](../cognitive-functions/pattern-integrator.md) identifies a platform workflow that would benefit from intelligence
2. [Analysis Partner](../agents/analysis-partner.md) evaluates feasibility and model options
3. [Architecture Explorer](../agents/architecture-explorer.md) designs the integration architecture
4. [Builder](../cognitive-functions/builder.md) + [Code Co-Creator](../agents/code-co-creator.md) implement the AI-powered capability
5. [Fresh-Eyes Observer](../cognitive-functions/fresh-eyes-observer.md) validates that the integration actually helps users

### AI Sophistication Advancement

1. [Analysis Partner](../agents/analysis-partner.md) assesses current AI task sophistication levels across platform
2. [Pattern Integrator](../cognitive-functions/pattern-integrator.md) identifies where simple automation could become contextual intelligence
3. [Builder](../cognitive-functions/builder.md) + [Code Co-Creator](../agents/code-co-creator.md) upgrade the capability
4. Measure sophistication level before and after advancement
