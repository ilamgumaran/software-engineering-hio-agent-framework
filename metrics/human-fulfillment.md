# Metrics: Human Fulfillment

## Purpose

Human wellbeing and growth metrics within the HIO model. These measure whether the transformation is making work better for people -- not just faster or more efficient, but more meaningful, challenging, and aligned with personal growth.

Human Fulfillment sits in Layer 3 (HIO) because it is a core differentiator of the HIO approach. Conventional transformations optimize for output. HIO optimizes for the humans producing that output.

If fulfillment degrades during transformation, the transformation is failing regardless of what the other metrics show.

---

## KPIs

| KPI | What It Measures | How to Capture | Target Direction | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intellectual Challenge | Does work stretch thinking? (1-10) | Anonymous self-report survey | Higher (>7) | Biweekly |
| Purpose Alignment | How connected does work feel to something meaningful? (1-10) | Anonymous self-report survey | Higher (>7) | Biweekly |
| Growth Trajectory | Are people expanding their capabilities? (1-10) | Anonymous self-report + manager conversation | Higher (>7) | Monthly |
| Anticipation Index | Do people look forward to work? (1-10) | Anonymous self-report survey | Higher (>7) | Biweekly |
| Autonomy Score | How much agency do people have over their work approach? (1-10) | Anonymous self-report survey | Higher (>7) | Biweekly |
| AI Collaboration Readiness | Comfort and enthusiasm for working with AI agents (1-10) | Anonymous self-report survey | Higher (>7) | Monthly |
| Identity Flexibility | Ability and willingness to shift between cognitive functions (1-10) | Self-report + rotation feedback | Higher (trending up) | Monthly |
| Psychological Safety | Willingness to take risks, raise concerns, admit mistakes (1-10) | Anonymous self-report survey | Higher (>8) | Monthly |
| eNPS | Employee Net Promoter Score | Standard eNPS survey | Higher (trending up) | Quarterly |
| Burnout Risk Index | Composite of energy, recovery, and overwhelm signals | Anonymous composite survey (3 sub-questions) | Lower (trending down) | Biweekly |

---

## Baseline Capture

Capture the Human Fulfillment baseline during Phase 0 using the Work Experience and Identity & Growth sections of the [baseline-survey.md](baseline-survey.md).

All survey data must be anonymous. People will not report honestly on fulfillment, burnout, or psychological safety if they believe their responses are traceable. Use a survey tool that guarantees anonymity and communicate that guarantee clearly.

---

## The Fulfillment Threshold

**If the average fulfillment score (across Intellectual Challenge, Purpose Alignment, Growth Trajectory, Anticipation Index, and Autonomy Score) drops below 7/10, trigger a retrospective deep-dive.**

This threshold exists because fulfillment is a leading indicator. By the time fulfillment drops are visible in delivery metrics, significant damage has been done to team health and retention risk.

The deep-dive should investigate:
- Which specific dimensions dropped and why
- Whether the drop correlates with specific transformation activities
- What the team needs to recover before proceeding

---

## Interpretation Guide

- **Intellectual Challenge** below 6 means work feels routine. Check whether AI agents are absorbing the interesting parts of work rather than the tedious parts. The goal is AI handles toil so humans tackle harder problems.
- **Purpose Alignment** below 6 suggests people have lost sight of why the transformation matters. Revisit the unit purpose (see [../cognitive-functions/](../cognitive-functions/)).
- **Autonomy Score** below 6 during transformation is a warning that the new structure feels more controlling than the old one. Cognitive units should increase autonomy, not decrease it.
- **AI Collaboration Readiness** below 5 means the team is not ready for deeper AI integration. Slow down AI sophistication progression and invest in education and paired experience.
- **Psychological Safety** below 7 blocks everything. People will not experiment with new workflows, rotate through cognitive functions, or report honest metrics if they do not feel safe.
- **Burnout Risk Index** trending up requires immediate action. Burnout during transformation is a structural failure, not a personal one.

## Connection to Other Categories

**Feeds:**
- Harmonization -- fulfilled humans collaborate more effectively with AI and each other
- Innovation -- fulfillment (especially intellectual challenge and autonomy) drives exploration
- SPACE/DX -- fulfillment and developer experience are deeply intertwined

**Fed by:**
- AI Utilization -- appropriate AI use frees humans for meaningful work, but inappropriate use can reduce fulfillment
- Current/Legacy -- sustainable velocity supports fulfillment; unsustainable velocity destroys it
- DORA -- deployment confidence reduces stress and improves anticipation

> **YOUR_ORG:** If you already run engagement surveys (Culture Amp, Lattice, Peakon, etc.), map your existing questions to the KPIs above. Do not create a parallel survey -- integrate HIO fulfillment questions into your existing pulse mechanism. Add any org-specific wellbeing indicators (remote work satisfaction, on-call burden perception, etc.) that matter for your context.
