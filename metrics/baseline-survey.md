# Baseline Survey

Anonymous survey template for capturing the HIO baseline. Run during Phase 0 (Weeks 1-2) before any structural changes begin. Results establish the starting point for all Layer 3 metrics and many Layer 2 metrics.

**Anonymity is non-negotiable.** Use a survey tool that guarantees anonymity. Communicate the guarantee explicitly. If people do not trust the anonymity, the data is worthless.

Estimated completion time: 15-20 minutes.

---

## Section 1: Work Experience

Rate each statement from 1 (strongly disagree) to 10 (strongly agree).

1. My work regularly challenges me intellectually -- I encounter problems that require real thinking, not just routine execution.
2. I feel a clear connection between my daily work and a purpose that matters to me.
3. I am actively growing my skills and capabilities in ways that are meaningful to me.
4. I have enough energy and recovery time to sustain my work without burning out.
5. I have meaningful autonomy over how I approach my work -- I choose methods, not just execute assignments.

**Open text:** What is the single biggest thing that would make your work experience better?

---

## Section 2: Developer Experience

Rate each statement from 1 (strongly disagree) to 10 (strongly agree).

1. I regularly get 4+ hours of uninterrupted focus time on most work days.
2. My development tools and infrastructure help me work effectively rather than getting in my way.
3. I rarely encounter friction or blockers that slow me down unnecessarily (waiting for reviews, broken builds, unclear requirements).
4. Code reviews happen quickly enough that they do not block my flow.
5. Our CI/CD pipeline is fast and reliable -- I trust it to catch problems and deploy safely.

**Open text:** What are your top 3 friction points in your daily development workflow?

---

## Section 3: AI Collaboration

Rate each statement from 1 (strongly disagree) to 10 (strongly agree).

1. I currently use AI tools (code completion, generation, chat, etc.) as a regular part of my workflow.
2. I feel comfortable and confident using AI tools to assist with my work.
3. I use AI for tasks beyond simple code completion -- including generation, analysis, or problem-solving.
4. I believe AI tools will significantly change how our team works over the next 6 months.
5. I am excited (not anxious) about deeper AI integration into our workflows.

**Multiple choice:** Which AI tools do you currently use? (Select all that apply)
- [ ] Code completion (Copilot, Codeium, etc.)
- [ ] AI chat for technical questions (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.)
- [ ] AI code generation from specifications
- [ ] AI code review or analysis
- [ ] AI for documentation
- [ ] AI for testing
- [ ] Other: [describe]
- [ ] I do not currently use AI tools

**Multiple choice:** What is your typical AI interaction sophistication? (Select the highest level that applies)
- [ ] L1: Autocomplete -- AI completes fragments of my work
- [ ] L2: Generation -- AI creates artifacts from my specifications
- [ ] L3: Analysis -- AI analyzes my work and recommends actions
- [ ] L4: Autonomous -- AI executes multi-step workflows I oversee
- [ ] L5: Co-creation -- AI and I produce outcomes neither could alone
- [ ] I do not use AI tools

---

## Section 4: Identity and Growth

Rate each statement from 1 (strongly disagree) to 10 (strongly agree).

1. My current role title and description accurately reflect the work I actually do and the value I provide.
2. I am comfortable contributing outside my primary role when the situation calls for it.
3. I would welcome the opportunity to develop capabilities in areas outside my current specialty.
4. I feel my career is progressing in a direction that is meaningful to me, not just the organization.
5. I can envision how my role might evolve to work more closely with AI tools and agents.

**Open text:** If you could expand your capabilities in any direction over the next 6 months, what would you choose and why?

---

## Section 5: Team Collaboration

Rate each statement from 1 (strongly disagree) to 10 (strongly agree).

1. I feel safe taking risks, raising concerns, and admitting mistakes on my team.
2. People on my team regularly contribute ideas and effort outside their primary role or specialty.
3. Our team has experienced moments where collaboration produced an outcome that no individual could have achieved alone.
4. Knowledge sharing happens naturally and frequently on my team -- people teach and learn from each other regularly.
5. I trust my teammates to support me when I try something new or unfamiliar.

**Open text:** Describe a recent moment when collaboration on your team produced something unexpected or especially valuable. If you cannot think of one, describe what would need to change for such moments to happen.

## Survey Administration Guide

### Before the Survey
- Communicate the purpose: establishing a starting point, not evaluating anyone.
- Guarantee anonymity explicitly. Name the tool. Explain that individual responses cannot be traced.
- Set expectations: the survey takes 15-20 minutes. It covers work experience, tools, AI, growth, and collaboration.
- Give people a 1-week window to complete it.

### After the Survey
- Share aggregate results with the entire org within 1 week of closing.
- Highlight strengths (high scores) as well as areas for growth (low scores).
- Connect the results to the transformation plan: "Here is where we are starting. Here is where we are going."
- Store the raw aggregate data as the Phase 0 baseline for comparison at Week 13 and Week 26.

### Repeat Schedule
Run the full survey at three points:
- Phase 0 (Weeks 1-2): Baseline
- Phase 2 gate (Week 13): Mid-transformation check
- Phase 4 close (Week 26): Final assessment

Between full surveys, the [weekly-harmony-pulse.md](weekly-harmony-pulse.md) captures lighter-weight fulfillment and collaboration signals.
