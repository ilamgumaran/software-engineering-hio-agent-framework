# Phase 3: Full Orchestration (Weeks 19-26)

## Goal

Transform the entire ~30-person org into cognitive units, achieve full measurement across all 9 metric categories, establish sustainable operating rhythms, and begin influencing the broader enterprise.

---

## Weeks 19-21: Dissolve Remaining Teams into Cognitive Units

### Formation of Final Units

- Remaining ~12-15 people join existing cognitive units or form **1-2 new units**
- Likely new units: **Intelligence Layer** and/or **Frontier** (see [../cognitive-units/](../cognitive-units/))
- Each new unit follows the compressed formation process from Phase 2 (1 week)
- Every new unit gets an assigned coach from the trained pool

### Handling the Remaining People

- Some people have been observing for 18 weeks — they're ready and eager
- Some people are skeptical but willing — pair them with the strongest coaches
- Some people are resistant — this is where the **invitational principle** is tested:
  - Have honest 1:1 conversations about concerns
  - Offer the chance to try for 2 sprints with an explicit opt-out
  - If someone genuinely doesn't want to participate, find a role that works — don't force it
  - Document what accommodation looks like so it's fair and consistent

### Coach-Supported Onboarding

- Coaches run formation workshops for each new unit
- Playbook v1 serves as the foundation — new units customize from there
- Embedded pioneers from Phase 1 or Phase 2 units provide peer support
- **Buddy system**: each new member pairs with someone who's been through the journey

### Dissolve the Old Structure

- By end of Week 21, **no traditional teams remain** — everyone operates through a cognitive unit
- Former team leads transition to coaches, **Pattern Integrator** function, or **Stakeholder Harmonizer** function
- Reporting structure adapts — people report to a manager, but work through their cognitive unit
- Be explicit: this is not eliminating management, it's separating management from work organization

---

## Weeks 22-24: Mature Operations

### Full HIO Dashboard

- All 9 metric categories tracking across all 4-5 cognitive units:
  - **Layer 1 (Current/Legacy)**: DORA, SPACE/DX, Platform Outcomes, Code Health
  - **Layer 2 (Harmonization Bridge)**: Innovation, Human Fulfillment, AI Utilization, Harmonization
  - **Layer 3 (Emergence)**: Emergence events, cross-unit insights, novel value creation
- **Metrics Monitor** agent provides automated weekly summaries to each unit and to leadership
- Comparison board shows full org trajectory from Phase 0 baseline to current state

### Cross-Unit Coordination

- Weekly cross-unit sync (30 minutes max) — **Stakeholder Harmonizers** from each unit attend
- Quarterly planning incorporates all units with shared priorities and dependencies
- **Architecture Explorer** agent supports cross-unit technical coherence
- Shared backlog for platform-wide concerns that span unit boundaries

### Operational Maturity Indicators

- Units self-facilitate all ceremonies without coach presence (coaches available on request)
- Function rotation happens naturally — people move between primary and secondary functions based on work needs
- AI agents are integrated into daily workflows, not a separate activity
- Emergence events are recognized and celebrated as they happen, not just logged retroactively

### Quarterly Rotation Mechanism

- Design and pilot the **quarterly rotation** process:
  - Each quarter, 10-20% of people can rotate to a different cognitive unit
  - Rotation is voluntary and based on growth goals, not performance
  - Prevents units from becoming siloed and builds cross-unit understanding
  - Coaches facilitate the transition to minimize disruption
- First rotation happens at Week 26 or shortly after

---

## Weeks 25-26: Enterprise Influence

### Share Learnings

- Prepare the **transformation case study**: metrics, stories, methodology, honest failures
- Present to peer engineering orgs — focus on evidence, not evangelism
- Offer to host observers from other orgs at cognitive unit ceremonies
- Share the playbook (now v2) with interested groups

### Document the Transformation

- Comprehensive retrospective covering all 4 phases
- Metric trajectory from baseline through each phase — the full story in data
- Lessons learned: what worked, what didn't, what you'd do differently
- Recommendations for the next org attempting this

### Plan Next Horizon

- What does HIO look like at 12 months? At 18 months?
- Identify areas for deepening: more sophisticated AI collaboration, cross-org cognitive units, platform-wide emergence patterns
- Plan the first quarterly rotation
- Set targets for the next quarter based on current trajectory

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] **All ~30 people** operating within cognitive units
- [ ] DORA metrics at **"High" or "Elite"** tier
- [ ] Layer 2 metrics (Innovation, Human Fulfillment, AI Utilization, Harmonization) **all improved** over baseline
- [ ] Layer 3 emergence targets met or **clearly trending** toward targets
- [ ] **Quarterly rotation mechanism** designed and first rotation scheduled
- [ ] **Playbook v2** published incorporating all 4 phases of learnings
- [ ] Full HIO dashboard operational with **automated reporting**
- [ ] Coaches operating independently — **sustainable without external support**
- [ ] At least **one other org** actively exploring HIO based on shared learnings
- [ ] Transformation case study documented for enterprise reference

## Risks in This Phase

| Risk | Trigger | Mitigation | Monitor |
|------|---------|------------|---------|
| Resistant individuals disrupt new units | Consistent negativity, refusal to participate | 1:1 coach conversations, offer genuine alternatives, don't force | Coach reports, unit fulfillment scores |
| Cross-unit coordination becomes bureaucratic | Meetings multiply, decisions slow down | Keep sync lightweight (30 min max), empower units to decide locally | Meeting time tracking, decision latency |
| Enterprise interest creates distraction | Too many observers, presentation requests | Designate 1-2 people as enterprise liaisons, protect unit focus time | Focus time metrics, unit feedback |
| Metrics gaming | Teams optimize for metrics rather than outcomes | Focus on metric combinations not individual numbers, use qualitative data as check | Metric correlation analysis, retrospective themes |
| Rotation mechanism destabilizes units | Too many people rotating at once | Cap rotation at 10-20% per quarter, stagger rotations, coach transitions | Unit performance during rotation periods |

---

### Organization Extension Point

> **YOUR_ORG:** The enterprise influence strategy depends heavily on your company's culture. In top-down orgs, secure executive sponsorship for sharing. In bottom-up orgs, let organic interest drive adoption. Adjust the quarterly rotation percentage based on your domain complexity — highly specialized domains may need slower rotation. Consider whether your org's performance review cycle aligns with the transformation timeline and plan accordingly.
