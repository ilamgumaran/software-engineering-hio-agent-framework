# Phase 2: Prove & Expand (Weeks 11-18)

## Goal

Prove the model works with data, expand from 1 cognitive unit to 3, train internal HIO coaches, and build the organizational momentum needed for full transformation.

---

## Weeks 11-12: Phase 1 Retrospective + Playbook v1

### Analyze Phase 1 Data

- Compile all metrics from 3 sprints across all 9 categories — compare every metric to baseline
- Identify which metrics improved, which stayed flat, and which regressed
- Map emergence events — what patterns produced them, what conditions enabled them
- Gather qualitative data: team interviews, retrospective themes, observer feedback

### Create Playbook v1

- Transform Playbook v0.1 (team's draft) into a structured guide covering:
  - How to form a cognitive unit (purpose, function mapping, working agreements)
  - Ceremony guides with timing, facilitation notes, and common pitfalls
  - AI agent pairing patterns that worked (and ones that didn't)
  - Identity grip patterns observed and how they were navigated
  - Measurement setup and dashboard configuration
- This playbook becomes the primary onboarding artifact for new units

### Present Results

- **Leadership briefing**: full comparison board walkthrough, honest about what worked and what didn't, recommendation to proceed with expansion
- **All-hands presentation**: pioneers share their experience in their own words — not a polished pitch, an honest account
- **Open Q&A**: address skepticism directly, share the hard parts alongside the wins
- Announce expansion plans and the invitation for the next wave

---

## Weeks 13-15: Form 2 New Cognitive Units

### Unit Selection

- Form 2 different cognitive units — recommend **Scale & Reliability** and **Developer Experience** (see [../cognitive-units/](../cognitive-units/))
- Choose units that address different platform challenges to demonstrate breadth
- Each new unit gets **8-10 people** — larger than the pioneer unit because coaches are available

### Pioneer-to-Coach Bridge

- 2-3 pioneers from Phase 1 serve as **embedded coaches** in the new units
- They co-facilitate the first sprint ceremonies alongside the new unit members
- They share the playbook but encourage each unit to adapt it to their context
- Pioneer unit continues operating independently — it doesn't stop to teach

### New Unit Formation (Repeat of Phase 1 Weeks 4-5, Compressed)

- **Day 1**: Unit purpose workshop — define why this unit exists and what it aims to achieve
- **Day 2**: Cognitive function self-assessment and mapping for all members
- **Day 3**: AI agent setup, pairing sessions, and workflow walkthrough
- **Day 4-5**: Working agreements, first sprint planning, and Sprint Kickoff
- Compressed from 2 weeks to 1 week because the playbook and coaches accelerate formation

### First Sprints for New Units

- New units run their first harmonized sprint (Weeks 14-15)
- Expect the same **15% productivity dip** — set this expectation with leadership again
- Embedded coaches facilitate ceremonies with decreasing involvement
- **Metrics Monitor** captures data from Day 1 for each new unit

---

## Weeks 13-15: Coach Development (Parallel Track)

### Select Internal HIO Coaches

- Identify **3-4 people** from the pioneer group and broader org (see [hio-coach-guide.md](hio-coach-guide.md))
- Selection criteria: natural facilitators, high emotional intelligence, respected by peers, genuinely excited about HIO
- Coaches are a **part-time role** — they continue contributing to their cognitive unit

### Coach Training

- Week 13: Shadow experienced facilitator running pioneer unit ceremonies
- Week 14: Co-facilitate new unit ceremonies with feedback
- Week 15: Solo facilitate at least one ceremony with observer feedback
- Training covers: purpose workshop facilitation, identity grip recognition, harmonization retrospective design, fulfillment signal reading, human-AI workflow coaching

---

## Weeks 16-18: Parallel Operation

### Three Units Running

- All 3 cognitive units running harmonized sprints independently
- Each unit has its own purpose, working agreements, and ceremony adaptations
- Cross-unit coordination established at a weekly cadence — lightweight sync, not bureaucracy

### Comparison Board

- Central dashboard tracking all 3 units against baseline across Layer 2 metrics
- Visible to the whole org — transparency builds trust and curiosity
- Include the pioneer unit's 7+ sprints of data showing the improvement trajectory
- New units' data shows they're following a similar (or faster) trajectory thanks to coaches

### Cross-Unit Learning

- Bi-weekly cross-unit showcase: each unit shares one emergence event or workflow innovation
- Coaches share patterns across units — what's working everywhere vs. unit-specific adaptations
- Begin identifying **cross-unit collaboration patterns** for Phase 3

### Playbook v1 Updates

- Incorporate learnings from new units — what in the playbook was wrong or incomplete
- Add coach-specific sections based on training experience
- Document unit-specific adaptations that worked

---

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] **3 cognitive units operational** with established cadences and working agreements
- [ ] Comparison board shows **improvement over baseline** across Layer 2 metrics for all units
- [ ] **3-4 coaches trained** and facilitating ceremonies independently
- [ ] **Playbook v1 published** with formation guide, ceremony guides, and coach guide
- [ ] New units have completed at least **2 harmonized sprints** each
- [ ] AI task sophistication trending toward **Level 2-3** across all units
- [ ] Rest of org expressing **curiosity, not resentment** — measured by pulse survey
- [ ] At least **4 additional emergence events** documented across new units
- [ ] Cross-unit coordination mechanism tested and working
- [ ] Leadership remains committed based on comparison board evidence

---

## Risks in This Phase

| Risk | Trigger | Mitigation | Monitor |
|------|---------|------------|---------|
| New units don't replicate pioneer success | Metrics flat or declining after 2 sprints | Review formation process, increase coach support, check if unit purpose resonates | Sprint-over-sprint metric trends |
| Pioneer unit stalls without attention | Pioneer metrics plateau or decline | Ensure pioneer unit has its own growth goals, not just coaching duty | Pioneer unit metrics continuation |
| Coach bandwidth insufficient | Coaches stretched between own work and coaching | Explicitly allocate 20-30% of coach time to coaching, reduce their delivery load | Coach fulfillment scores, unit facilitation quality |
| "Us vs them" dynamics emerge | Non-unit members express frustration or resentment | Share everything openly, invite observers, ensure non-unit work is valued equally | Org-wide pulse survey, informal feedback |
| Expansion pace too fast | Units forming before coaches are ready | Slow down — exit criteria trump dates, delay new unit formation if needed | Coach readiness assessment |

---

### Organization Extension Point

> **YOUR_ORG:** Choose which 2 cognitive units to form based on your platform's biggest pain points. If reliability is your top concern, start with **Scale & Reliability**. If developer adoption is lagging, start with **Developer Experience**. Consider forming units that serve different downstream customers to maximize visible impact across the org. Adjust coach allocation based on your org's facilitation culture — orgs with strong existing facilitation may need fewer dedicated coaches.
