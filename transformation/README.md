# Transformation Guide

## Overview

26-week accelerated HIO transformation for a ~30-person platform engineering org. This operational guide covers 4 phases with exit criteria that trump dates — if criteria aren't met, phases extend rather than rushing forward.

The transformation converts a traditional team-structured org into one operating through **cognitive units** where humans exercise multiple **cognitive functions** alongside **AI agents**, measured across **9 metric categories** spanning delivery, experience, and emergence.

---

## The 4 Phases

| Phase | Name | Weeks | Goal |
|-------|------|-------|------|
| 0 | Seed | 1-3 | Baseline, alignment, pioneer selection |
| 1 | First Cognitive Unit | 4-10 | Form first unit, run 3 harmonized sprints |
| 2 | Prove & Expand | 11-18 | Prove with data, form 2 more units, train coaches |
| 3 | Full Orchestration | 19-26 | All 30 people in units, full measurement, enterprise influence |

## Key Principles

- **Exit criteria trump dates** — extend phases if criteria aren't met, never rush
- **Measure from Day 1** — all three metric layers captured from the start
- **Invitational, not forced** — people choose to participate, nobody is assigned
- **Pioneer group leads by example**, not by mandate or authority
- **Identity grip is natural** — recognize it, work with it, don't fight it
- **15% temporary productivity dip** is expected and communicated to leadership upfront
- **Emergence is the goal** — outcomes that exceed what humans or AI could achieve alone

---

## Prerequisites

- Leadership buy-in for a 6-month transformation with explicit commitment to not pull the plug during the expected dip
- Budget for AI tools (**Claude Code**, **GitHub Copilot**, etc.) across all participants
- Dedicated HIO coach time (3-4 people, part-time through Phase 2, more active in Phase 3)
- Willingness to measure **human fulfillment** alongside delivery metrics
- Existing CI/CD pipeline with accessible DORA metric data (or willingness to instrument)
- Security team engaged on AI tool approval before Week 3

---

## End-State Targets (Week 26)

- All 30 people operating in cognitive units
- DORA metrics at "High" or "Elite" tier
- Time from ask to experiment reduced 40%+ from baseline
- Self-service rate > 70%
- Downstream team NPS improved 15+ points
- Rework rate reduced 25%+
- Defect escape rate reduced 20%+
- Developer focus time at 3+ days/week
- AI task sophistication at Level 3+ average
- 8+ documented emergence events per quarter
- At least one other org exploring HIO
