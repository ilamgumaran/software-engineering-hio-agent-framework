# Phase 2: Prove and Expand (Weeks 11-18)

## Strategic Intent

Scale from one cognitive unit to three, train HIO coaches, and establish cross-unit knowledge sharing. Phase 2 is where the framework proves it works beyond a single team and begins building organizational muscle memory. The comparison board becomes the primary recruiting tool.

---

## Timeline

| Week | Focus | Key Activities |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Week 11-12** | Coach training | Train 2-3 HIO coaches, begin second unit formation |
| **Week 13-14** | Second unit launch | Second cognitive unit operational, third unit forming |
| **Week 15-16** | Third unit launch | Third unit operational, cross-unit sync established |
| **Week 17-18** | Metric maturity | All 9 categories automated, agent sophistication advancing to L3 |

---

## Key Deliverables

| Deliverable | Definition of Done |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **3 operational cognitive units** | 15-18 people in HIO units running harmonized sprints |
| **Trained HIO coaches** | 2-3 coaches capable of facilitating unit formation and sprint ceremonies |
| **Cross-unit knowledge sharing** | Weekly 30-minute sync happening consistently |
| **Full metric automation** | All 9 metric categories collecting without manual intervention |
| **Agent maturity L3** | At least 2 units with agents operating as proactive collaborators |
| **Legacy transition plan** | Written plan for converting remaining teams in Phase 3 |

---

## Exit Criteria

Phase 2 is complete when:

1. Three cognitive units are operational with distinct outcome focuses
2. At least 2 HIO coaches are trained and actively facilitating
3. All 9 metric categories are reporting automatically
4. The comparison board shows consistent improvement across multiple units
5. A transition plan exists for remaining legacy teams

**If exit criteria are not met by Week 18:** Extend Phase 2 by 2 weeks. Assess whether coach capacity or volunteer pipeline is the bottleneck.

---

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Second unit does not replicate first unit's success | Each unit is different -- coach helps adapt, not copy |
| Coach training insufficient | Pair new coaches with experienced first-unit facilitators |
| Cross-unit overhead | Keep sync to 30 minutes, pattern-sharing only -- no status updates |
