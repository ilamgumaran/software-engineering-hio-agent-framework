# Phase 3: Full Orchestration (Weeks 19-26)

## Strategic Intent

Complete the transformation by converting the remaining teams into cognitive units 4 and 5. Transition from coach-led facilitation to self-optimizing units. Establish the sustainability model that will persist beyond the 26-week transformation window.

---

## Timeline

| Week | Focus | Key Activities |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Week 19-20** | Final unit formation | Convert remaining teams into cognitive units 4 and 5 |
| **Week 21-22** | Self-optimization | Units begin adjusting their own sprint cadence and agent configurations |
| **Week 23-24** | Org-wide dashboard | Full 9-category dashboard live at organization level |
| **Week 25-26** | Sustainability | Document operating rhythms, conduct transformation retrospective |

---

## Key Deliverables

| Deliverable | Definition of Done |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **5 cognitive units operational** | All ~30 people organized into outcome-focused units |
| **Agent maturity L4** | Agents operating as collaborative partners across all units |
| **Org-wide dashboard** | 9-category metrics visible at unit and organization level |
| **Self-optimizing workflows** | Units modifying their own cadences based on metric feedback |
| **Sustainability model** | Documented rhythms for ongoing operation beyond Week 26 |
| **Transformation narrative** | Full story captured by Documentation & Knowledge agent |

---

## Exit Criteria

Phase 3 -- and the transformation -- is complete when:

1. All 5 cognitive units are operational
2. DORA metrics are at Elite or High tier
3. Average fulfillment score is above 8.0
4. Agent utilization is at L4 across all units
5. Emergence events average 2+ per unit per sprint
6. The sustainability model is documented and being practiced

**If exit criteria are not met by Week 26:** The framework continues operating. Schedule a 4-week extension focused on the unmet criteria. The transformation is not "failed" -- it is still converging.

---

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Final teams resist conversion | Evidence from 3 units should be compelling; do not force -- extend timeline instead |
| Self-optimization leads to drift | Metrics Monitor flags when unit metrics diverge from healthy ranges |
| Sustainability model not adopted | Embed rhythms into existing calendar infrastructure, not separate systems |
