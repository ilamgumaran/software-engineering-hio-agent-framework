# Working Agreements

> Shared commitments that define how cognitive units operate. Universal agreements apply to all units. Each unit adds its own agreements during formation.

---

## Universal Agreements

These apply across every cognitive unit in the organization:

1. **No fixed roles for tasks** -- collectively decide who (human or AI) takes each function
2. **Thinking time is work time** -- analysis, reflection, and design are valued outputs
3. **AI outputs are starting points, not final answers** -- human judgment shapes every decision
4. **Measure outcomes, not hours or story points** -- impact over activity
5. **Deep work blocks are protected** -- no meetings, no Slack during designated focus time
6. **Fulfillment matters** -- if it drops below 7, we address it as a unit
7. **Emergence is celebrated and documented** -- unexpected breakthroughs get shared broadly
8. **Identity grip is natural** -- we observe it without judgment
9. **Rotate quarterly to expand bandwidth** -- try new functions, new units, new perspectives
10. **Share everything openly with the broader org** -- no knowledge hoarding across units

## Agreement Lifecycle

### Creating Agreements

- Any unit member can propose a new agreement at any time
- New agreements are discussed at the next unit standup or async in the unit channel
- Adoption requires **unit consensus** -- not majority vote, full alignment

### Reviewing Agreements

- Revisit all agreements at each **harmonization retrospective**
- Ask: Is this agreement still serving us? Should it be modified or retired?
- Track which agreements generate the most discussion -- they may need refinement

### Changing Agreements

- Any member can propose a change at any retrospective
- Changes require **unit consensus**
- Changed agreements are communicated to the broader org for transparency

### Retiring Agreements

- Agreements that no longer serve the unit are retired, not ignored
- Retired agreements are logged with a brief note on why they were removed
