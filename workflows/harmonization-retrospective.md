# Workflow: Harmonization Retrospective

## Trigger

Immediately follows the [Sprint Outcome Review](sprint-outcome-review.md) at the end of each 2-week sprint. This is not a standard retrospective. It focuses on emergence, growth, and fulfillment.

---

## Participants

| Role | Human Functions | AI Agents |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Facilitator | [Stakeholder Harmonizer](../cognitive-functions/stakeholder-harmonizer.md) | -- |
| Growth input | [Growth Catalyst](../cognitive-functions/growth-catalyst.md) | -- |
| Full unit | All members | -- |
| Capture | -- | [Documentation & Knowledge](../agents/documentation-knowledge.md) |

## Core Questions

The retrospective is structured around six questions. Each gets dedicated time.

1. What emergence events happened this sprint?
2. Where did we default to old patterns (scrum habits, command-and-control, story points)?
3. Where did identity grip show up (clinging to roles, resisting function rotation)?
4. How is everyone growing?
5. How are we using AI (as tool, as partner, or somewhere in between)?
6. What is one experiment we will try next sprint?

---

## Steps

### Phase 1: Emergence Reflection (10 minutes)

1. **Facilitator**: Prompt the group: "What emerged this sprint that nobody planned for?"
2. **All members**: Share emergence events. Moments of unexpected insight, value created through human-AI collaboration, or patterns that appeared organically.
3. **[Pattern Integrator](../cognitive-functions/pattern-integrator.md) functions**: Connect this sprint's emergence to patterns across previous sprints.

### Phase 2: Pattern Defaults (10 minutes)

4. **Facilitator**: Prompt: "Where did we fall back into old patterns?"
5. **All members**: Identify moments of defaulting to status updates, story point thinking, assignment-based work, or treating AI outputs as final answers.
6. **[Fresh-Eyes Observer](../cognitive-functions/fresh-eyes-observer.md) functions**: Name patterns others might not see. What felt normal but was actually a regression?

### Phase 3: Identity and Growth (10 minutes)

7. **Facilitator**: Prompt: "Where did identity grip show up? How is everyone growing?"
8. **All members**: Reflect on moments of clinging to familiar roles or resisting new cognitive functions. Share growth edges.
9. **[Growth Catalyst](../cognitive-functions/growth-catalyst.md) functions**: Highlight growth moments observed during the sprint. Where did someone stretch into a new function?

### Phase 4: Fulfillment Check (10 minutes)

10. **Each member**: Rate fulfillment on a 1-10 scale. Share the reasoning behind the number.
11. **Facilitator**: If anyone scores below 7, the group pauses to discuss. What would raise it? What needs to change?
12. **[Resonance Sensor](../cognitive-functions/resonance-sensor.md) functions**: Sense the emotional tone beneath the numbers. Is anyone performing fulfillment without feeling it?

### Phase 5: AI Collaboration Assessment (10 minutes)

13. **All members**: Reflect on AI usage this sprint. Where did AI agents genuinely augment human work? Where did we over-rely or under-use them?
14. **[Learner](../cognitive-functions/learner.md) functions**: What did we learn about effective human-AI collaboration this sprint?

### Phase 6: Experiment Commitment (10 minutes)

15. **All members**: Propose experiments for next sprint. One change to how the unit works, collaborates, or uses AI.
16. **Facilitator**: The group converges on exactly one concrete experiment. Define what it is, how to try it, and how to know if it worked.
17. **[Documentation & Knowledge](../agents/documentation-knowledge.md)**: Capture all reflections, fulfillment scores, and the committed experiment.

---

## Inputs

- Sprint Outcome Review results
- Sprint emergence log
- Previous retrospective experiment results
- Individual fulfillment baselines from sprint kickoff

## Outputs

- Documented emergence events and pattern observations
- Fulfillment scores with trends across sprints
- One concrete experiment for next sprint with success criteria
- Growth observations per member (private if preferred)

---

## Anti-Patterns

- **Skipping the fulfillment discussion.** Low fulfillment is the most important signal. Never cut it for time.
- **Blaming instead of observing.** "We fell back into old patterns" is an observation. "Person X forced us into standups" is blame.
- **Not committing to an experiment.** The retrospective must produce exactly one experiment. Vague intentions do not count.
- **Running it like a standard retro.** This is not "what went well / what went poorly." It is about emergence, growth, and identity.
- **AI agents in the room.** This is a human-only reflection space. AI captures notes but does not participate in the discussion.
- **Performing fulfillment.** Scoring 8/10 when you feel 5/10 undermines the entire process. Psychological safety is prerequisite.
