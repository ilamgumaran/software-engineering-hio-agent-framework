# Workflow: Exploration Time

## Trigger

Weekly allocation of 3-4 hours for unknown-outcome work. Connected to the unit's purpose but deliberately outside current sprint outcomes.

---

## Participants

| Role | Human Functions | AI Agents |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| All members | All cognitive functions | All unit agents (on request) |

---

## Rules

1. **3-4 hours per week**, distributed however the individual prefers (one block or multiple sessions).
2. **No deliverable required.** Exploration that leads nowhere still has value if the learning is shared.
3. **Connected to unit purpose** but not constrained by current sprint outcomes. Adjacent problems, emerging technologies, alternative approaches, and deeper understanding all qualify.
4. **Can be individual or collaborative.** Pair exploration and group spikes are encouraged.
5. **AI agents are available** for exploration support. Use [Analysis Partner](../agents/analysis-partner.md) for research, [Code Co-Creator](../agents/code-co-creator.md) for prototyping, [Architecture Explorer](../agents/architecture-explorer.md) for design exploration.
6. **Must share learnings.** Every exploration produces at least one emergence log entry, shared during the next [Daily Harmony Check](daily-harmony-check.md) or [Harmonization Retrospective](harmonization-retrospective.md).
7. **Can become sprint outcomes.** If exploration reveals something promising, it can be proposed as a sprint outcome in the next [Sprint Kickoff](sprint-kickoff.md).

---

## What Exploration Looks Like

- Investigating alternative architectures or revisiting past decisions with fresh data
- Prototyping new human-AI collaboration patterns or technologies the unit has not used before
- Reading research papers relevant to the unit's domain
- Building proof-of-concept implementations for ideas that surfaced during deep work
- Pairing with someone from another cognitive unit to cross-pollinate ideas

---

## How to Track: Emergence Log

Exploration produces entries in the unit's emergence log. Each entry captures:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Date | When the exploration happened |
| Explorer(s) | Who participated |
| Topic | What was explored |
| Key Finding | One sentence: what did you learn? |
| Potential | None / Worth Watching / Propose for Sprint |
| AI Agents Used | Which agents supported the exploration |

The [Documentation & Knowledge](../agents/documentation-knowledge.md) agent maintains the emergence log and surfaces patterns across entries over time.

---

## Steps

### Before Exploration

1. **Individual**: Choose a topic connected to unit purpose. No approval needed.
2. **Optional**: Post a brief note in the unit channel so others can join if interested.

### During Exploration

3. **Explorer(s)**: Follow curiosity. Use AI agents as thought partners, not just tools.
4. **[Learner](../cognitive-functions/learner.md) functions**: Stay in learning mode. Document surprising findings as you go.

### After Exploration

5. **Explorer(s)**: Write an emergence log entry (one paragraph minimum) and share at the next daily check or retrospective.
6. **If promising**: Propose the finding as a potential sprint outcome for the next [Sprint Kickoff](sprint-kickoff.md).

---

## Inputs

- Unit purpose and domain context
- Previous emergence log entries and curiosity surfaced during sprint work

## Outputs

- Emergence log entries and shared learnings
- Potential sprint outcome proposals (when applicable)

---

## Anti-Patterns

- **Canceling exploration for sprint work.** Exploration is not a buffer for overcommitment. Protect it.
- **Requiring deliverables.** The moment exploration needs a demo or report, it becomes sprint work.
- **Not sharing learnings.** Exploration without sharing is just personal time. The unit learns together.
- **Restricting topics.** As long as it connects to unit purpose, any direction is valid.
- **Treating it as free time.** Exploration is intentional curiosity, not a break.

---

### Organization Extension Point

> **YOUR_ORG:** Adjust weekly allocation based on sprint intensity. Define how exploration proposals flow into your backlog or outcome planning process. Consider cross-unit exploration sessions for broader innovation.
