# Workflow: Deep Work and Collaboration Rhythm

## Trigger

Daily structure for every working day within a harmonized sprint.

---

## Participants

| Role | Human Functions | AI Agents |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| All members | All cognitive functions | All unit agents |

---

## Daily Structure

| Block | Duration | Purpose |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Deep Work | 4 hours | Protected focus time. No meetings, no Slack, no context-switching. |
| Collaboration Windows | 2 hours | Human-AI-human interaction, pairing, reviews, discussions. |

---

## Deep Work Block

### Rules

1. **4 hours minimum**, uninterrupted. This is non-negotiable.
2. **No meetings** may be scheduled during deep work. No exceptions for "quick syncs."
3. **No Slack/chat** monitoring. Notifications are silenced. Urgent issues use a dedicated escalation channel only.
4. **No context-switching.** Each person works on one outcome stream during deep work.
5. **AI agents work asynchronously** during deep work. Members queue requests for [Code Co-Creator](../agents/code-co-creator.md), [Analysis Partner](../agents/analysis-partner.md), or [Architecture Explorer](../agents/architecture-explorer.md) and review outputs during collaboration windows.

### What Deep Work Looks Like

- [Builder](../cognitive-functions/builder.md) and [Solution Architect](../cognitive-functions/solution-architect.md) functions: writing code, designing systems, exploring architectural options
- [Problem Framer](../cognitive-functions/problem-framer.md) and [Learner](../cognitive-functions/learner.md) functions: thinking through problem spaces, studying new domains, building mental models
- **Thinking time is work time.** Staring at a whiteboard counts. Walking and thinking counts.

---

## Collaboration Windows

### Rules

1. **2 hours daily**, scheduled as a contiguous block when possible.
2. **Human-AI-human interaction** is the primary mode. Pair programming with AI, reviewing AI outputs together, discussing AI-surfaced patterns.
3. **Pairing and mobbing** happen here. Cross-pollination between outcome streams.
4. **Code reviews** happen here, not asynchronously in PRs that sit for days.
5. **AI agent review**: Check outputs from agents that worked during deep work. Accept, reject, or refine.

### What Collaboration Looks Like

- Pair programming with [Code Co-Creator](../agents/code-co-creator.md) while another member observes and challenges
- Reviewing [Quality Analyst](../agents/quality-analyst.md) findings or [Architecture Explorer](../agents/architecture-explorer.md) proposals as a group
- Cross-stream knowledge sharing and resolving stuck points from the [Daily Harmony Check](daily-harmony-check.md)

## Schedule Patterns

Units choose the pattern that fits their energy:

### Pattern A: Morning Deep Work

| Time | Block |
|------|-------|
| 9:00 - 9:15 | Daily Harmony Check |
| 9:15 - 13:15 | Deep Work |
| 13:15 - 14:00 | Lunch |
| 14:00 - 16:00 | Collaboration Windows |

### Pattern B: Afternoon Deep Work

| Time | Block |
|------|-------|
| 9:00 - 9:15 | Daily Harmony Check |
| 9:15 - 11:15 | Collaboration Windows |
| 11:15 - 12:00 | Lunch |
| 12:00 - 16:00 | Deep Work |

Units may adjust times but must preserve the 4-hour deep work block and 2-hour collaboration window.

---

## Anti-Patterns

- **Scheduling meetings during deep work.** "Just a quick 15-minute sync" fragments focus and costs far more than 15 minutes.
- **Using collaboration windows for solo work.** Collaboration time is for interaction. Solo work belongs in deep work.
- **Skipping AI agent review.** If agents worked during deep work, their outputs need human review during collaboration.
- **Always choosing the same pattern.** Experiment with both schedules. Energy varies by person and season.
- **Treating deep work as optional.** When sprint pressure rises, deep work is the first thing cut and the last thing that should be.
