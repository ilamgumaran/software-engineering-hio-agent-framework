# Workflow: Daily Harmony Check

## Trigger

Every working day, at the start of the collaboration window. This is NOT a status update.

---

## Participants

| Role | Human Functions | AI Agents |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Facilitator | Rotates daily among unit members | -- |
| Metrics input | -- | [Metrics Monitor](../agents/metrics-monitor.md) |
| All members | All cognitive functions | -- |
| Capture | -- | [Documentation & Knowledge](../agents/documentation-knowledge.md) |

---

## Core Questions

The Daily Harmony Check answers three questions only:

1. **Where is energy flowing?** What work has natural momentum right now?
2. **Where are we stuck?** What feels blocked, unclear, or draining?
3. **What should shift?** Should any work move between members or agents?

---

## Steps

### Phase 1: Metrics Pulse (2 minutes)

1. **[Metrics Monitor](../agents/metrics-monitor.md)**: Present overnight metrics and alerts. Pipeline failures, anomalous patterns, outcome progress indicators. Keep it factual and brief.

### Phase 2: Energy Round (5 minutes)

2. **Each member**: Share energy state in one sentence. Not what you did yesterday, but where your energy is right now. Examples: "Energized, deep in the auth redesign." "Stuck on the data migration, losing momentum." "Curious about the pattern I found during exploration."
3. **[Resonance Sensor](../cognitive-functions/resonance-sensor.md) functions**: Notice patterns across the group. Is energy concentrated or scattered? Is anyone isolated?

### Phase 3: Shift Decisions (5 minutes)

4. **Facilitator**: Surface stuck points from Phase 2. For each: can an AI agent unblock it? Should another member pair on it? Should we deprioritize it?
5. **All members**: Decide on any work shifts. Reassign AI agents, form pairs, or adjust scope as needed.
6. **[Pattern Integrator](../cognitive-functions/pattern-integrator.md) functions**: Connect dots across stuck points. Are multiple blocks related to the same root cause?

### Phase 4: Capture (2 minutes)

7. **[Documentation & Knowledge](../agents/documentation-knowledge.md)**: Record any shifts made, stuck points flagged, and energy patterns observed. Update the sprint's emergence log if relevant.

### Phase 5: Transition (1 minute)

8. **Facilitator**: Confirm the check is complete. Members transition to deep work or collaboration as scheduled.

---

## Inputs

- [Metrics Monitor](../agents/metrics-monitor.md) overnight alerts and metrics
- Each member's current energy state
- Previous day's shift decisions (if any)

## Outputs

- Documented energy patterns for the day
- Shift decisions (work reassignments, pairing changes, AI agent reallocation)
- Updated emergence log entries (if applicable)

---

## Anti-Patterns

- **Status reporting.** Listing what you did yesterday and plan to do today. Focus on energy, not tasks.
- **Exceeding 15 minutes.** If discussion is needed, schedule a follow-up. The check is a pulse, not a meeting.
- **Ignoring energy signals.** When someone says they are stuck or drained, that is data. Act on it.
- **Skipping the check.** Consistency builds trust. Even a 5-minute abbreviated version is better than skipping.
- **Problem-solving in the check.** Identify stuck points but solve them afterward during collaboration windows.
- **Solo AI agents not mentioned.** If an AI agent is blocked or producing unexpected output, surface it here.

---

### Organization Extension Point

> **YOUR_ORG:** Adjust timing based on unit size. For distributed teams, consider async harmony checks where members post energy states before a shorter synchronous sync.
